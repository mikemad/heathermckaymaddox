Welcome

I'm glad you're here.

This space exists as a gentle landing place: a place to pause, breathe, and reconnect with what is unfolding within you. Here, your story is honored. Your inner life is approached with care. And you are met with curiosity, compassion, and respect for the pace of your journey.

My work integrates contemplative-evocative spiritual direction, faith-informed narrative therapy, and psycho-spiritual care to offer guidance to those seeking healing, clarity, and renewed direction. Together, we attend to your lived experience of your sacred story.

The Work

My Approach

My approach weaves together five interlocking streams of care — each attending to a different dimension of the whole person.

01

Re-Storying

Supporting you in making meaning of your experiences, noticing old narratives shaped by survival or loss that don't fit anymore, and allowing a truer, more life-giving story to emerge naturally.

02

Spiritual Direction

Contemplative and evocative listening as you explore your relationship with God, within your inner life — your questions, your unknowns, and the invitations unfolding for your life.

03

Trauma-Informed Soul Care

Honoring how experience is held in the body and nervous system, cultivating safety, regulation, and compassion as foundations for healing and integration.

04

Psycho-Spiritual Insight

Gently holding the interconnectedness of mind, body, and spirit, informed by interpersonal neurobiology — honoring your lived experience without labeling, supporting awareness, integration, and compassionate self-reflection.

05

Creative & Contemplative Practices

Using prayer, reflective writing, poetry, imagination, and silence to awaken insight, deepen awareness, and nourish the inner life.

About

At the threshold
of your story.

Most people arrive carrying more than they can name — a story unfinished. I meet them at the threshold of where the story is asking to be told, to be heard, for Truth to find its voice.

I am a spiritual director and mentor devoted to spiritual formation, inner healing, and relational transformation. I accompany individuals and groups in listening deeply to their lives — attending to the places where prayer, experience, and relationship meet — so that the wisdom God has already placed within each person can be named, trusted, and lived from.

I serve as mentor and faculty with the CenterQuest School of Spiritual Direction, where I have taught Contemplative Prayer and Theological Reflection, and continue to walk alongside emerging directors in their formation.

I have always been drawn to story. Formed by my Gaelic and Celtic roots, where music, poetry, and ancestral memory carry meaning, I learned early that listening itself is a reverent act. This way of listening continues to guide my vocation, particularly as people find themselves at profound life-changing thresholds — marked by transition, desire, longing, loss, or trauma.

Alongside spiritual direction, my life's work has unfolded in healthcare and education. For more than thirty years, I have worked with individuals and families living with illness, grief, trauma, mental health, and profound change. I currently teach BSN students at the University of Washington.

At heart, my calling is to cultivate spaces where people feel seen, known, and safe enough to tell the truth of their lives — spaces where spiritual formation and neuroscience meet with humility, compassion, and grace, and where fuller freedom in Christ is gently invited.
